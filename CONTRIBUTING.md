## Why Not Open Source?

InvestScape™ represents **hundreds of hours of research, validation, and development** across three major domains:

1. **Financial Calculations (E1–E11)** — Validated against FCAC stress-test requirements, OSFI guidelines, and CMHC insurance standards
2. **Advanced Finance & Tax (E12–E28)** — Jurisdiction-specific rules for CRA (Canada) and IRS (US), commercial real estate methodologies, tax optimization algorithms
3. **Market Research & Construction (E29–E45)** — Neighborhood composite scoring, construction project management, development budgeting

**This is our competitive moat.** Keeping InvestScape™ proprietary allows us to:
- Build a sustainable business (InvestScape.app SaaS)
- Invest in continuous validation and compliance
- Maintain multi-jurisdiction accuracy
- Protect your data privacy
- Offer professional-grade financial tools

If we open-sourced this, competitors would immediately copy our work and undercut our SaaS offering. That would harm both our ability to innovate and your ability to access professional real estate investment analysis tools.

### Option 2: Report Bugs or Issues
Found a bug in the calculations? Have a suggestion?

1. Open an Issue on GitHub
2. Include:
   - The engine name (e.g., "E5: Returns Calculator")
   - Input values
   - Expected output vs. actual output
   - Jurisdiction (Canada/US) and property type (RES/COM/DEV)

We will review and respond to all issues within 5 business days.

### Option 3: Request a Feature
Want to see a new calculation engine or data source integrated?

1. Open an Issue with the label `feature-request`
2. Describe the use case and how it would help you
3. Prioritize this for Licensor's product roadmap consideration
